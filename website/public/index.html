( 00 ) Idea

Windows tells you things with toasts that stack in a corner and vanish before you read them. Onyx gives every live thing one place, at the top of the screen, where your eyes already go. It grows when something matters and shrinks back to a sliver when nothing does.

( 01 ) Now playing

Whatever’s playing, right there.

Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music in a background Firefox tab. Onyx finds the song, pulls its colours from the cover and keeps the bars moving to the beat. Videos and voice notes stay out of it.

  • Two players at once
  • Remembers your last song
  • Music only, by design

( 02 ) Hover

Hover, and it opens up.

Point at the island and it opens into a full player: cover, seek bar, skip, volume and a jump to the app. Move away and it folds back. You never leave the window you were working in.

Try it: hover the island at the top of this page and press play.

  • Play, pause, skip, seek
  • Per-app volume
  • Scroll for widgets

( 03 ) Voice typing

Hold a key. Speak. Let go.

Hold Right Ctrl and talk. Your words land in whatever you’re typing in, cleaned up: stutters, “um”s and second thoughts are gone, and spoken lists come out as bullet points. The speech model runs on your PC. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  • Works in any app
  • On-device, works offline
  • Optional local AI rewrite

Your agents tap you on the shoulder.

Coming next: when Claude Code needs permission, the question will appear in the island, so you can answer without hunting for the terminal. When it finishes, you’ll know, even with your editor buried under three other windows.

  • Allow or deny in place
  • Done, at a glance

( 05 ) Widgets

Your machine’s pulse.

Scroll inside the open island to find live CPU, GPU, memory, network and temperature, plus a calculator that respects operator precedence. When the CPU pegs, the pill’s edge starts to glow, so you can see it from across the room.

  • Read once a second
  • Only while open
  • Hot-CPU glow

( 06 ) Dynamic

It knows when to move.

When an app needs the top of the screen for its tabs or toolbar, the island glides down to the bottom edge. When the space clears, it comes back. It checks a two-pixel ring around itself, so it never jumps for nothing.

  • Top, bottom or dynamic
  • Follows your cursor across monitors
  • Never covers your tabs
